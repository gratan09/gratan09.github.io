Research Interests
Where I want to take this next

My research centers on deep learning and multimodal representation learning for language and speech, with the goal of extending these methods to visual modalities — images and video. Prior work across three areas has given me a foundation in both learned and engineered representations:

I
ML Security & Robustness
Structured, engineered-feature detection for adversarial domains like Android malware — where a model has to stay accurate as attackers adapt around it.
II
NLP & Multimodal Language
Fine-tuning and benchmarking transformer models (BERT, RoBERTa, DistilBERT) for misinformation classification — a step toward learned language representations.
III
Speech & Affective Computing
CNNs over acoustic features — MFCCs, chroma, spectral contrast — to recognize emotion from raw speech, my entry point into audio representation learning.

Going forward, I want to investigate how language, audio, and visual information can be aligned and fused to improve generalization, interpretability, and reliability in real-world settings — building AI systems that learn from heterogeneous data and transfer knowledge across modalities.

Hybrid Approach for Android Malware Detection Using Machine Learning
Independent research · IEEE TDSC manuscript, under review
May 2026
  • Investigated whether combining permission-based static features with a multi-stage detection architecture improves robustness over single-stage classifiers.
  • Applied Gradient Boosting feature importance for feature selection; benchmarked KNN, Random Forest, Decision Tree, and Naïve Bayes on the Drebin Android Malware Dataset.

Automated Misinformation Detection Using Pre-trained Transformer Models
Independent research
Feb 2026
